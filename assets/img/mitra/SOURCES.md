# Logo sources

Organization marks in this folder are shown only to identify organizations listed as prospective contacts. Their presence does not imply partnership or endorsement.

Most PNG files are favicon images retrieved from Google's favicon service for the corresponding organization's official website domain:

`https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain={official-domain}&sz=128`

The domain is recorded in the corresponding organization entry in `app.js`. The following files are sourced from Wikimedia Commons instead:

- `adhi-co-id.png`: [Adhi Karya.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Adhi_Karya.png), public-domain text logo; original credit references ADHI's 2018 annual report.
- `atrbpn-go-id.png`: [Logo BPN-KemenATR (2017).png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Logo_BPN-KemenATR_(2017).png), public-domain Indonesian government logo.
- `big-go-id.png`: [Badan Informasi Geospasial logo.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Badan_Informasi_Geospasial_logo.png), public-domain text logo credited to BIG.

If a verified image source is unavailable, the interface keeps the organization's initials rather than substituting an unverified mark.
